How to get your bearer token
  1. Open GuruWalk and sign in to your company account normally.
  2. In Firefox or Zen, open Developer Tools, then Storage → Cookies. In Chrome, use Application → Cookies.
  3. Select the GuruWalk site and find the GuruWalk authentication cookie. Its value is your bearer token; copy only that value.
  4. Paste it into the private field above and click Save and verify. Clear the clipboard afterward.

This is the current manual fallback. Ask an administrator if you cannot find the cookie. One-click reconnect is not available yet; automatic token renewal has not been verified.

GuruWalk is read-only: syncs do not change anything there. Imported guests do not receive welcome messages automatically. Local attendance and contact edits are preserved. Guide assignments remain manual.
